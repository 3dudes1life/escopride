CHOOSE HOW YOU SHOW UP

Three partner types.
One trusted network.

Businesses and organizations may qualify for more than one category.

🌈01
PRIDE PARTNER

Show visible support year-round.

For storefronts and service businesses ready to visibly support the community year-round, with optional Pride Month activations.

  • Offer a special, event, product, donation, or meaningful community benefit when it fits your business
  • Receive a verified listing in the app
  • Display the official Pride Partner decal or digital badge
  • Optionally activate Pride Passport check-ins
🛡️02
WELCOMING PLACE

Provide an affirming physical location.

For locations where LGBTQ+ people and allies can expect respectful treatment and visible support.

  • Maintain a welcoming, nondiscriminatory environment
  • Ensure staff understand what the badge represents
  • Display the Welcoming Place decal
  • Offer reasonable basic assistance when someone needs a public place to pause or seek help

The badge represents a commitment to welcome and assist; it is not a guarantee against every possible incident.

💻03
ONLINE PARTNER

Support the community without a storefront.

For online shops, creators, wellness providers, consultants, artists, and remote services.

  • Receive a verified online profile in the app
  • Display the official digital partner badge
  • Link the badge to your verified Esco Pride profile
  • Optionally offer a year-round or Pride Month promotion or benefit

COMMUNITY STANDARDS

The badge has to mean something.

Participation is reviewed and may be paused or removed when a business no longer meets the commitment.

01

Welcome respectfully

Treat customers, employees, guests, and community members with dignity regardless of sexual orientation, gender identity, or gender expression.

02

Stand behind the badge

Owners, managers, and customer-facing staff should understand what Esco Pride participation represents.

03

Keep details current

Maintain accurate hours, offers, links, accessibility information, and participation status.

04

Address concerns

Work in good faith with Esco Pride if a serious harassment or discrimination concern is reported.
