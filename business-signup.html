HOW YOU CAN PARTICIPATE

Three partner types. One trusted network.

Businesses may qualify for more than one category.

🌈
PRIDE PARTNER

Actively participate in Pride Month.

For storefronts and service businesses that want to visibly support the community and take part in the June campaign.

  • Offer a Pride Month special, event, product, donation, or meaningful benefit
  • Display the Esco Pride Partner decal or digital badge
  • Receive a verified app listing
  • Optional Pride Passport check-ins
🛡️
WELCOMING PLACE

Provide an affirming physical location.

For locations where LGBTQ+ people and allies can expect respectful treatment and visible support.

  • Maintain a welcoming, nondiscriminatory environment
  • Ensure staff understand what the badge represents
  • Display the Welcoming Place decal
  • Offer reasonable basic assistance when someone needs a public place to pause or seek help

The badge represents a commitment to welcome and assist; it is not a guarantee against every possible incident.

💻
ONLINE PARTNER

Support the community without a storefront.

For online shops, creators, wellness providers, consultants, artists, and remote service businesses.

  • Display the Esco Pride badge online
  • Receive a verified online listing in the app
  • Link the badge to the official business profile
  • Optional Pride Month promotion or benefit

THE OFFICIAL MARK

A badge customers can recognize and verify.

Physical partners may place the badge in a storefront window. Online businesses may use it on their website, shop, or booking page.

Digital badges will link back to the business's current Esco Pride profile so customers can confirm participation.

COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Participation means more than displaying a rainbow.

01

Welcome respectfully

Treat customers, employees, guests, and community members with dignity regardless of sexual orientation, gender identity, or gender expression.

02

Stand behind the badge

Owners, managers, and customer-facing staff should understand what Esco Pride participation represents.

03

Keep details current

Maintain accurate hours, offers, links, accessibility information, and participation status.

04

Address concerns

Work in good faith with Esco Pride if a serious harassment or discrimination concern is reported.

COMMUNITY MEETUPS

Help give LGBTQ+ Escondido places to gather.

Hosting is optional. Participating businesses can share availability through the future dashboard, and Esco Pride will rotate locations based on capacity, accessibility, age restrictions, and scheduling.

Some months may include two meetups: one all-ages gathering and one additional adult social event.

✓ Rotated among participating businesses
✓ Based on submitted availability
✓ All-ages access prioritized
✓ Announced through the app
