Privacy Notice

How Esco Pride handles information on the current website and during early partner recruitment.

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This notice covers the current website and early business application process. The mobile app privacy policy will be expanded before public beta and app-store release.

Information the public website collects

The public landing page does not currently require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information through a website database.

Business application drafts

The partner application can save a draft in your browser's local storage. That draft remains on the device and browser you used. Esco Pride does not receive the draft merely because it was saved.

Submitting an application

When you choose “Prepare application email,” the site opens your device's email application with the information you entered. The application is not received until you review and send that email. Your email provider's privacy practices also apply.

Information we may receive

If you email us, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, business details, website or social links, address, participation preferences, accessibility information, and any other details you choose to provide.

How information is used

  • Review and respond to partner applications.
  • Verify and prepare approved business profiles.
  • Coordinate badges, Pride Month offers, passport participation, meetups, and app onboarding.
  • Maintain safety, accuracy, and community standards.

Sharing

We do not sell business applicant information. Information may be shared with service providers or contractors only when reasonably necessary to operate Esco Pride, and approved public profile details may later appear in the app with the partner's knowledge.

Retention and choices

You may ask to correct or delete information you submitted by contacting will@outatinc.com. Drafts stored locally can be removed with the “Clear draft” button or by clearing site data in your browser.

Children

The current business application is intended for adults authorized to represent a business or organization. The future app's age-related terms and protections will be published before launch.

Changes

This notice may be updated as website forms, analytics, account features, or the mobile app are introduced. The updated date at the top will reflect material revisions.
