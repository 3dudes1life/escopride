UPLOAD LOCATION
Upload/extract the CONTENTS of this ZIP directly into your domain's public_html (or the document root for escopride.com).
This package is intentionally root-ready: index.html sits at the top level.

BEFORE ANNOUNCING
1. Confirm https://escopride.com loads without a certificate warning.
2. Confirm https://www.escopride.com redirects to https://escopride.com.
3. Open Business Portal and submit a test application through your own mail app.
4. Verify the test email goes to admin@outatinc.com and has an "Esco Pride - ... Application" subject.
5. Test Privacy and Terms links.
6. Share https://escopride.com in a private Facebook message first and confirm the social preview artwork/title appear.
7. Test on iPhone once over cellular, not only Wi-Fi.

NOTE
The website is intentionally static. Business applications and Founding Beta requests open the visitor's email app; nothing is silently submitted by the site.
