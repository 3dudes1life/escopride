WHY ESCO PRIDE

We should not have to leave our city to feel seen.

Esco Pride begins with something practical: helping LGBTQ+ people and allies identify the local businesses willing to visibly welcome us—and giving those businesses a community ready to support them back.

We are starting with trusted partners, rotating meetups, and a useful app. Bigger celebrations can grow from a community that already knows how to find each other.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

What to know before launch.

When will the Esco Pride app launch?+

The first public launch is planned around Pride Month 2027, with business recruitment and community previews beginning beforehand.

Will the app be free?+

The core app experience—business discovery, Avi Trails, the Pride Passport, and meetup information—is planned as a free community resource.

Who can become an Esco Pride Partner?+

Escondido-area storefronts, service providers, welcoming community spaces, online businesses, creators, and organizations may apply. Participation is reviewed rather than automatic.

Is Esco Pride a parade or festival?+

Not at launch. Esco Pride is beginning as a year-round local network and app. Larger public celebrations may grow later from a strong, connected community.
